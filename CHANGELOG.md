# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - Unreleased
- The main window opens inside the work area of the monitor it starts on, so a small or high-scaling display, or a size saved on a bigger monitor, shows all of it, and changing the app size keeps it on its own monitor.
- Icon-only buttons give screen readers the name from their tooltip, and controls reached with the keyboard show a thin solid focus ring in the theme accent. Keyboard focus moves into the shortcuts, About and settings cards when they open, stays there until they close, and then returns. Tab skips hidden links. Empty text and the hidden parts of scroll bars no longer read as items on screen. Each group of option buttons is one Tab stop that lands on the selected option, and the arrow keys move between its options.

- Added a tray app that captures with Print Screen. Plain Print Screen repeats the last mode used, Alt+Print Screen captures the active window, Ctrl+Print Screen captures the monitor under the pointer, and Shift+Print Screen captures every monitor.
- Added region capture over a frozen copy of the desktop on every monitor, so menus and tooltips stay in the picture. Escape or a right-click cancels with nothing copied.
- Added window capture through Windows Graphics Capture, including covered windows, with rounded corners filled from the desktop behind them.
- Captures go straight to the clipboard as PNG and as a standard bitmap, and the last capture can be saved as a PNG from the tray menu.
- The tray icon reports when another program or a Windows setting already owns Print Screen and links to that setting.
- Added the editor with pen, highlighter, arrow, rectangle, ellipse, text, opaque redaction and crop, plus select and move, undo and redo, Fit and 100% zoom, and exports at the capture's exact pixel size.
- Added Open, Save, Save as and Copy, and a Recent menu of the latest captures.
- Added the capture library: every capture is kept in one encrypted file (SQLCipher, AES-256) that opens with your Windows account, or with a password set in Capture settings. With no image open the window shows it as shelves of carousels, one big carousel, a grid or a timeline, by date or by collection, with search across window titles, program names, notes and the text in each picture. Ctrl+L shows it over an open image. Captures can be moved into collections, noted, copied, saved as files or deleted, and Keep captures limits the history by count or age. The old Recent folder is imported into it on first start.
- The library shows Windows' Screenshots folder as a collection read live from its files, with Open, Copy, Show in File Explorer and Add to the library. Capture settings can turn it off.
- Added the collection strip: with a picture open, its collection runs beside it as a carousel at the top or bottom, or a column of thumbnails at the side, so the next picture is a click or an arrow key away. Alt+Shift+arrow docks it to that side, and the arrow of its own side minimizes it. The rail sits between the strip and the picture, a column beside a side strip or a row beside a top or bottom one, and its chevron minimizes and restores the strip.
- Added view buttons to the footer, as KillerPDF has: the library's four views (F5 to F8) and its grouping, which bring the library up over an open picture, and with a picture open a collection strip button, whose flyout lists the sides with their shortcuts and the strip's views, and zoom out, zoom level and zoom in.
- The title bar shows the library's name on the right, where Killendar and KillerNotes show their open database, centered on the caption buttons, and an open file's name after the wordmark, as KillerPDF shows it.
- Captures sit on dark cards in the library grid and timeline, and on gray cards in the collection strip, which now sits on the window's own background instead of a pane. Card color in a capture's menu picks the card color with the family color picker, and Collection color in a collection's menu gives a collection a color that marks its heading and its cards.
- Added a rolodex view for a strip docked at the side: the captures are cards on a turning drum, the current one rising out in front at full size and the others leaning back above and below it, as an alternative to the thumbnail list.
- Added capture settings with recorders for the four global shortcuts, and options to open the editor after each capture and show a notification.
- Added Start with Windows to the About card. It starts KillerShot in the notification area when you sign in, and stays off until you turn it on.
- Added a Close to system tray option to the About card. With it off, closing the window quits KillerShot.
- Added the family window, rail, toolbar styles, all 13 themes with Dark and Teal as the default, 19 languages, the F1 shortcuts card with a keyboard map, and the F12 About card with a hardware or software rendering choice.
- Added Customize toolbar to reorder the toolbar's buttons, move them between its sides or into the More menu, and add or remove separators. Buttons that do not fit a narrow window wait in the More menu, Alt+M hides the toolbar, and Ctrl+Shift+1 to 6 pick the toolbar style.
- Added the KillerShot app icon to the exe, taskbar, title bar, dialogs, About card and tray.
- Added after-capture choices to copy to the clipboard, save straight to an output folder (Windows' Screenshots folder by default), ask where to save, or open the editor, with a file name pattern, PNG or JPEG with a quality setting, and a capture delay.
- Added options to include the mouse pointer, play a camera sound, and pick the window to capture with a click. Hovering in region mode highlights a window and a single click takes it.
- Added printing from the editor (Ctrl+P) and the tray, with shrink, enlarge and rotate options.
- Added screen recording to MP4 (H.264, through the encoder built into Windows) with optional system sound, microphone or both, or animated GIF, with frame rate and quality settings for each, a video settings card on the rail, a Record button (Ctrl+R) and a global shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+Print Screen) that starts and stops it.
- Recording picks a region, window or monitor, counts down, and shows a frame and a control bar with Pause, Stop and Discard that never appear in the video. GIFs store only what changed between frames, with a palette per frame.
- Menu separators draw their divider line instead of leaving an empty gap.
- Added a command line (KillerShot.exe --cli, or KillerShot.Cli.exe in a console) that captures a region, rectangle, window, monitor or every monitor, records MP4 or GIF for a set time, lists, searches, copies, exports and opens captures in the library, lists monitors and windows, and shows the settings in use, with JSON output and exit codes for scripts and KillerMCP.
- KillerShot.exe --open opens an image in the editor, handing it to a running KillerShot when one is open.
- KillerShot.exe --demo opens a scratch library filled with screenshots of the Killer Tools apps from code\Demo\KillerShot, with its own settings and Screenshots folder, beside a running KillerShot.
